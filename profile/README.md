# MCE - Mechatronics & Computational Society

**MCE (Mechatronics & Computational Society)** is a student engineering organization focused on robotics and applied technology.

Our projects span competitive robotics, research and development, and software development.

## Projects

### 🤖 MCE-Vex

Software for our two VEX U V5 competition robots, **Alpha** and **Beta**. Includes autonomous routines, drivetrain control, mechanisms, and driver control.

### 🤖🏥 MCE-RRandD

Our research and development repository for **Mitch**, a medical-purpose robot. 

### 🌐 MCE-Website

The organization's website, providing information about MCE, our projects, members, and activities.
